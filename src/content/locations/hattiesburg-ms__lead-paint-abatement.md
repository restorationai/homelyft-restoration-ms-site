**Lead paint abatement in Hattiesburg** usually comes up for one of two reasons: a pre-1978 home is about to go through a renovation, or a rental property manager needs a property cleared before new tenants move in. Hattiesburg's housing stock spans Forrest and Lamar counties and includes a meaningful share of homes built well before the 1978 federal lead paint ban, which means window sashes, trim, and old exterior siding are the first places we check before any remodeling work starts.

## Why Hattiesburg Properties See Lead Paint Issues

A lot of Hattiesburg's older neighborhoods were built in the decades when lead-based paint was standard on both interior trim and exterior siding. Combine that with Mississippi's humid, swing-season climate, hot summers followed by damp falls and winters, and you get paint that cracks, chalks, and sheds dust faster than it would in a drier region. Window wells and door frames are usually the worst offenders because they take the most friction from daily use. Once paint starts chalking on an exterior wall, lead dust can settle into soil around the foundation, which is a separate concern from the interior work but one we flag during inspection.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Hattiesburg

Every job starts with testing, not assumptions. We take paint chip or XRF-style samples from trim, sills, doors, and any surface scheduled for sanding, scraping, or demolition. If lead is confirmed, we seal the work area with poly sheeting and negative air containment before any disturbance begins, following EPA Lead Safe Certified work practices for setup, containment, and cleanup verification. Dry sanding and open-flame paint stripping are off the table entirely; we use wet methods, HEPA-filtered vacuums, and chemical stripping where appropriate to keep dust from traveling into the rest of the house. After abatement, we run a visual clearance check and wipe sampling before the containment comes down, so the homeowner isn't left guessing whether the space is safe to reoccupy.

## Hattiesburg Insurance & HOA Coordination

Lead abatement is rarely a straightforward insurance claim unless it's tied to another covered loss, like fire or water damage that exposed old painted surfaces underneath. When that's the case, we document the lead findings alongside the primary damage so the paperwork lines up for the adjuster. For rental properties and HOA-governed homes in Hattiesburg, we also provide the clearance documentation landlords need before re-occupancy, since Mississippi rental disclosure rules follow the federal lead paint notification requirements for pre-1978 units.

## Local note

One thing we watch for on older Hattiesburg homes: exterior paint that looks fine from the ground but is heavily alligatored up near the soffits and second-story trim, where sun exposure and humidity cycles hit hardest. Homeowners often underestimate how much dust that higher, less visible paint sheds during routine gutter cleaning or pressure washing, so we recommend testing those areas even when the ground-floor trim looks solid.
