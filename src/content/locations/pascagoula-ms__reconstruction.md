## Why Pascagoula Properties See Reconstruction Needs

Jackson County sits low and close to the water, and Pascagoula's building stock reflects decades of hurricane seasons. Many homes have already been rebuilt once since Katrina, and others still carry original framing and plumbing from before current wind-load codes existed. Storm surge, wind-driven rain intrusion, and the occasional fire from lightning or electrical faults in older wiring are the events that most often trigger a full reconstruction scope here, rather than a simple drywall repair. Humidity near the Pascagoula River and the Gulf shoreline also means any structure left open after damage can develop secondary rot or mold in wall cavities faster than it would further inland, which changes how quickly demo and drying have to happen before rebuild can start.

## Our Reconstruction Services Process in Pascagoula

We start with a structural assessment that separates what can be dried and saved from what has to come out, since over-demoing adds cost and under-demoing invites callbacks. From there, our crews handle selective demolition, framing repair, insulation, drywall, flooring, trim, and paint, along with roofing and exterior work when wind or water has compromised the building envelope. Because we hold Fortified roofing certification for wind-resistant construction, roof and exterior reconstruction on Pascagoula homes is built to the wind-uplift standards that matter most during the next named storm, not just to pass a basic inspection. Interior work follows IICRC-based drying and reconstruction sequencing, so framing and subfloor moisture content are verified before closing up walls, which matters in a climate where trapped humidity behind new drywall is a common cause of early repeat damage.

## Pascagoula Insurance & Flood Coordination

Most Pascagoula rebuilds involve two separate claims: a homeowners policy for wind or fire damage and a National Flood Insurance Program policy for surge or rising water, since standard homeowners coverage typically excludes flood. We document damage with the photos, moisture readings, and scope breakdowns adjusters expect for both policy types, and we itemize structural reconstruction separately from contents or mitigation so the claim isn't bundled in a way that slows reimbursement. If your property sits in a FEMA-mapped flood zone, elevation certificates and local permitting requirements can affect what's required for rebuild, and we factor that into the scope before work starts rather than discovering it mid-project.

## Local note

Soil near the coast and along the Pascagoula River tends to be sandy and holds water close to the surface, which means slab and foundation work here doesn't always behave the way it would on firmer inland ground. We also see faster corrosion on exposed metal fasteners and HVAC components in homes within a few miles of the shoreline, so reconstruction specs for those properties often call for stainless or coated hardware even when the original materials weren't. It's a small detail, but using standard fasteners on a coastal rebuild is one of the more common reasons a repair fails early.
