## Why Pass Christian Homes Carry Lead Paint Risk

Pass Christian's housing stock skews old by Gulf Coast standards, with a meaningful share of homes built well before the 1978 federal ban on lead-based residential paint. Add the area's humid subtropical climate, and you get a maintenance pattern that keeps lead paint relevant decades after it was last applied: high humidity and salt-laden air accelerate chalking and peeling on old exterior paint, and every major storm that has rolled through Harrison County since has forced repairs that disturb those old painted surfaces. One thing we watch for on older Pass Christian homes: paint that looks stable on the surface often hides multiple older layers underneath, especially on exterior trim and window sashes that have been repainted repeatedly since Katrina-era rebuilding.
