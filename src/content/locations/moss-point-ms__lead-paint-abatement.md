## Why Moss Point Homes Often Carry Lead Paint Risk

Moss Point's older neighborhoods, many built before the 1978 federal ban on residential lead paint, were finished with oil-based coatings that held up well against Gulf Coast humidity for decades. That durability is part of the problem now. As those coatings age, chalk, and crack under repeated humidity cycles off the Pascagoula River and the Sound, the paint film breaks down into fine, friable chips and dust rather than large peeling flakes. That dust settles into window wells, porch floors, and garden soil around the foundation, which is exactly where kids and pets spend time. Window sashes and exterior siding tend to show the worst wear first because they take the most direct sun and moisture exposure.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Moss Point

We start with testing, either XRF analysis or lab-submitted paint chip samples, so you know which surfaces actually contain lead before any work plan is written. If abatement is needed, we isolate the work area with poly sheeting and negative air where the scope calls for it, following EPA RRP work practices for wet scraping, HEPA vacuuming, and controlled demolition rather than dry sanding or open-flame paint removal, both of which aerosolize lead dust. Debris is bagged and disposed of per hazardous waste handling rules, and we do a final HEPA-vacuum and wet-wipe clearance before we call a room finished. For homes going through renovation or a window replacement project, we coordinate the lead-safe work practices with whatever reconstruction or remodeling scope you've already got planned, so the abatement doesn't become a second disruptive project later.

## Local note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Moss Point specifically: the combination of river humidity and salt air off the Sound makes old paint film go brittle and friable faster than it would further inland, so chips that look intact on the surface can crumble the moment they're touched. We factor that into how aggressively we contain a work area here, especially on exterior siding and porch ceilings, where paint that looks stable during the initial walkthrough can start shedding dust within minutes of light scraping.
