**Rebuilding after storm or fire damage in Biloxi requires more than carpentry.** Reconstruction Services from HomeLyft Restoration MS means putting a structure back together the way the Mississippi Gulf Coast actually requires: elevated framing where flood codes demand it, wind-rated materials where hurricane exposure is a given, and permitting handled correctly the first time so Harrison County inspectors don't send you back to the drawing board.

## Why Biloxi Properties See Reconstruction Needs

Biloxi sits on a low, flat peninsula between the Mississippi Sound and Back Bay, and that geography shapes almost every rebuild we take on. Storm surge and wind-driven rain from Gulf systems are the most common triggers, but slow leaks and fire damage in older slab-on-grade homes built before modern wind codes create just as much structural work. Humidity near the coast keeps lumber and subfloor moisture content high year-round, which means a rebuild has to account for materials that dry slower and swell faster than they would further inland. Post-Katrina rebuilding across the coast also pushed many structures toward elevated foundations and reinforced connections, so reconstruction here often means matching new framing to code requirements that didn't exist when the original structure went up.

## Our Reconstruction Services Process in Biloxi

Every rebuild starts with documenting what's salvageable and what isn't, photographed and logged for the insurance file before a single stud comes out. From there our crews handle structural framing, subfloor and wall replacement, insulation, drywall, and finish work, matching materials and fasteners to current Mississippi wind-load requirements where the scope calls for it. On fire-related rebuilds, we coordinate directly with the teams handling fire damage restoration and smoke damage restoration so reconstruction doesn't start until the structure is clean and dry per the IICRC S500 standard. For storm-related losses, that same coordination happens with our storm damage restoration and emergency board-up and tarping crews, so the property is weather-tight the same day demolition finishes, not days later.

## Biloxi Insurance & Permitting Coordination

Coastal Mississippi rebuilds almost always touch flood insurance, wind insurance, or both, and the two carriers rarely talk to each other. We itemize structural reconstruction scope separately from contents and finish work so adjusters on either policy can process their portion without duplicate paperwork. Permitting through Harrison County or the City of Biloxi's building department is part of the job, not an add-on, and that includes verifying base flood elevation requirements on any structure being substantially rebuilt. We carry Fortified Home wind-storm certification, which matters when a carrier or inspector wants proof that roofing and structural connections meet the stricter coastal wind standard, not just the baseline code.

## Local note

One detail that catches out-of-town contractors off guard: a lot of Biloxi housing stock near the water sits on piers or elevated slabs from post-Katrina rebuilds, while blocks further inland still have original 1960s-70s slab construction. That mix means two houses on the same street can require completely different foundation approaches during reconstruction, so we verify elevation and foundation type on-site before finalizing a structural scope rather than assuming based on the neighborhood.
